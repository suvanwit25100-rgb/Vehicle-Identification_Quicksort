SECTION — 01

FROM FOOTAGE
TO EVIDENCE

  1. 1

    Track

    Vehicles are detected and followed through the clip, so every frame of one car stays together.

  2. 2

    Locate

    A plate detector finds the plate in each frame and crops it.

  3. 3

    Read

    Each character is read on its own, and how sure the reader was is kept.

  4. 4

    Fuse

    Frames vote slot by slot. A slot they cannot agree on stays unknown.

  5. 5

    Match

    The pattern is matched to the registry, scored with colour and body type, and explained line by line.
