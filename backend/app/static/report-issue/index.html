    What happens to your report

    • The photo, plate, place and time go to officers of the department that handles this kind of report. Nobody else sees them.
    • Owner details are never shown to you or to anyone who is not an officer.
    • Time and place hidden in a photo are only suggested to you. You decide what to send.
    • Please photograph the vehicle, not people. Do not follow or confront anyone.
    • This is a demonstration build. Do not use it for an emergency: call 112.
